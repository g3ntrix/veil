Installing Veil

1. Drag Veil into the Applications folder.
2. Open Veil from Applications.

If macOS says Veil "cannot be opened" or "is damaged":

Veil is not notarized by Apple. Notarization needs a paid Apple Developer account, which this project does not
have. The app is ad-hoc signed, and you install it at your own risk.

  a. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  b. Scroll down to the message about Veil and click "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password.
     (On recent macOS, right-click then Open no longer skips this check.)

If there is no "Open Anyway" button, open Terminal and run:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Veil.app

Then open Veil again.
